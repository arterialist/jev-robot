# Contributor conduct

Treat contributors with respect when discussing the robot demo, including when you disagree about a result, design choice, or implementation.

Discuss the work directly. Explain criticism with evidence and give others room to correct mistakes. Do not harass, threaten, demean, or discriminate against contributors. Sexual harassment, targeted abuse, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents this project.

The maintainer, [@arterialist](https://github.com/arterialist), may edit or remove inappropriate content, close a discussion, or restrict participation. The response should reflect the severity and pattern of the behavior. Technical disagreement in good faith is welcome.

Use GitHub's [reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) to report abuse privately. Do not repeat private information in a public issue. The maintainer should respect the privacy of people who report a concern.
